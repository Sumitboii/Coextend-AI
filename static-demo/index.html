Grounded RAG Knowledge Base & Strict Guardrails

        

          The system indexes Coextend's 14 proprietary corporate documents into ChromaDB embeddings. When analyzing a prospect, internal capabilities are cited strictly from verified RAG chunks.
        


        

          

            Anti-Hallucination Charter

            Accreditation Standards
            
Outputs reference CWCT testing guidelines, BS EN 13830 curtain wall standards, ASTM, AAMA, and CSI MasterFormat standards.

          

          

            Human-in-the-Loop Safe
            
Zero automated outreach sending. LinkedIn message generation remains under 300 characters, formatted strictly for manual sales review.
