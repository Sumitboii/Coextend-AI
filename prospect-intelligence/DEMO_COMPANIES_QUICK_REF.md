## Copy-Paste Instructions

1. **Pick a company** from the table above
2. **Open** http://localhost:8000
3. **Paste company name** into "Company Name" field
4. **Paste website URL** into "Website" field
5. **Click** "Start Research"
6. **Watch** status update (🟡 yellow → 🟢 green)
7. **Review** the research brief, score, and findings

## What You'll See

- 📊 **Lead Score**: 0-100 with 5-tier ICP classification
- 📄 **Research Brief**: Company overview & findings
- ✅ **Source Verification**: Which sources were used (and rejected)
- 💼 **CRM Export**: HubSpot data format with priority tier
- 📧 **Outreach Drafts**: Email & LinkedIn templates
